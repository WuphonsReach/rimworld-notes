## Rocks / Sand / Dirt Recipes

"Grind rocks into sand (bulk)" takes 5x rocks and produces 5x sand.  This should produce 25x sand from 8x rocks.

"Grind sand into clay (bulk)" takes 5x sand and produces 5x clay.  This should produce 15x clay from 10x sand.
