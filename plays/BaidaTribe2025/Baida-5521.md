# Baida Tribe

# Starting Conditions

- Size 4 of 5 world.  One click to the right of the middle.
- Boreal forest, but mixed biome with increased animal life feature.
- Limestone, granite and sandstone.
- Cold: -30C to +11C (-9.7C avg)
- Only a 10 day growing period, Jul 1st to Jul 11th.
- Nearest friendly settlement is to the west along a roadway.
