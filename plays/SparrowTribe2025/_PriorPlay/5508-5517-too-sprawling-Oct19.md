- Planet Seed: guts
- Mid-sized planet, 100% coverage
- Biome: Boreal Forest (cirque)
- Avg Temp: -1.5C (-21C to 18C)

- Nearby Civ: Thaen (neutral) with 3 outposts that were seen

## Ideology (Ilwaban Creed)

- Elder worship
- Likes to wear rings ("Jewelry" mod)
- Elder likes to wear tribal headdress

## Psycast Trees

- Protector: Doctors.  Can eventually regrow limbs.
- Empath: Good for leaders.  Casts "Word of X" (joy, etc.), "Psychic soothe (gender)", "Bliss", "Mind wipe".
- Technomancer: Enchant quality of items.  Haywire (EMP) psycast.  Rock and steel constructs for hauling.
- Wildspeaker: Summon or affect animals.

# 5508

Note that we start in 5508 because of the "Persistent" date mod.  There's been a few other colonies before I settled in.

- Purple fell in love with a visitor from another tribe.
- Elder role granted to Green for conversion and rituals.

## Jul-14-5508

Completed the first (20) anima grass at the anima tree.  This will let Green start down the Empath psycast tree and unlock "Word of Joy".

The shattered empire sends their first tribute collector.  We'll ignore them as we have no gold yet.

Food harvesting went well, with almost 800 nutrition stored away.  But 225 of that is "Plant Scraps" which will be used to make kibble and compost.  The rest is a mix of smoked meat, cooked meals, pemmican, raw crops and other foods.

## Sep-5508

Red is made Chief of the tribe.  That ceremony will be followed by the first Fair of Age annual celebration.

First blizzard on the 8th.  But since we're starting to settle in for the winter season it won't be a big deal.  We have about 900 nutrition stored away with about 280 of that being plant scraps.  Figure 5 colonists at 2/day and we need 600 per year.  If wild animals wander close, we might go hunting this winter.

Bulk traders arrived and we traded some clothing for components.  That will be enough to get us through the tribal era.  The only thing I need them for is the auto-vents that are temperature controlled.  We settled around a steam geyser and that room gets up to 50C at times.  With the auto-vents, I can configure it to only let heat out of the room if the barracks and workshop areas are too cold.

Green gets their next anima tree ceremony.

## Dec-5508

Most of the initial setup is done.  There are no crops to sow, no animals to hunt.  Therefore it's time to start in on research to unlock stone cutting and complex furniture.  But since I use the "tech advancing" mod that makes future tiers 30x to 50x more expensive, we must first work through the rest of the starting tier technologies.

For next winter, we'll build a maze for the wildlife with an animal bowl full of plant scraps in the middle.  That will bring the animals to us for easy hunting and stocking up the larder.

Felicity (wanderer event) shows up and wants to join.  She's going to have a -14 near permanent mood debuff due to being difficult to convert.  Plus the "Persecution complex" trait is likely to add another negative mood post-conversion.  We're going to turn around and banish her and take the mood hit in the colony.

Snip shows up.  A Fire Jotun with chemical fascination and pathogenic traits.  I think we're going to ask her to leave.  She didn't take that well and now we're going to get raided.  Fortunately it was an easly dispatched sight stealer.

Bad news, a blizzarisk clutch mother.  We have to venture out and dispatch that threat before it multiplies.  Purple picked up a bit of frostbite in his right arm, leg and lung.  But he has two of those and will be seeking medical treatment.

There are at least (2) ancient danger locations on the map.  One in the southwest corner and one in the southeast corner.  We'll need to setup extra walls around those once we unlock stone blocks.

# 5509

## Apr-5509

She made it inside and the aves are dead.  Now to work on converting her to our ideology.  Green managed to convert her on the first attempt!  

Shemi has Animal Warcall as a baseline ability due to her genes.  It has a five day cooldown and summons animals.  Her learning growth towards the age 10 tier was solid out of the gate.  So she'll get to choose from three options at age 10.

We also managed to capture a breeding pair of devil sheep that wandered onto the map.  If the growing seasons go well, I'll consider expanding the size of the flock.  But right now it's very strict with only 1 adult male and 1 adult female being allowed.

Shemi picks up the tranquil trait at age 10.  Because of her genes (and the Big and Small mod, I think), she's really tiny compared to the baseline colonists.

## Jul-5509

Midway through the summer.  Wealth is about 97k, nutrition floats at around 1050.  Of that nutrition value, 390 is the 7800 plant scraps in storage.  That leaves about 500 nutrition for 6 pawns at 2/day or about 42 days of food.  The little "Food Alert" mod says that I have 19 days, but it only counts simple meals and better.

Shemi is at about 81% on the "Learning" bar.  Looking at the [Growth Tiers and Rewards table](https://www.rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Children#Growth_score), the last column gives the minimum learning score over time to hit the various tiers for trait picks and passion picks.  Therefore, 81% is not bad.  We have no ability yet to build school desks and blackboards, so that's probably the best we can do.

For psycasters, I should be able to start in on Purple soon.  He'll be going down the Protector to gain healing abilities.  Including the ability to regrow lost limbs.  Green needs a few more points to learn all of the Empath psycast spells.  One trick to getting Green to level up faster is to have him casting "Psychic Sooth (gender)" on cooldown.  The downside is that he's going to spend a lot of time meditating at the anima tree.

Finished all of the neolithic technology research, time to unlock the medieval period technologies.  The first two are "Complex Furniture" and "Stonecutting".  Then we'll start in on things like tree planting, coal heating, smithing, jewlery making, etc.

Iuliia joins after being rescued from a crashed escape pod and healed up.  She is age 50 and a baseliner.  Strong passion for mining.  Regular passions for shooting, melee, cooking, animals and social.  She has the "Graver" trait which means she's fine around corpses and will wear tained apparel without qualms.  Green now needs to start converting her over to our ideology.  Her mood is super low, but Green can cast "Word of Serenity" to stop any mood break and put her to sleep for a while.

Torrential rain at the end of Jugust.  This causes localized flooding.  But it's really good for the pea plants.

Looks like it will take four or five attempts for converting Iuliia to our ideology.  She has about eight negative mood debuffs due to her current ideology.  Her mood averages 45-50%, which is not great.

Purple unlocks psycasting abilities at the anima tree.

Eep.  Our adult female devil sheep has the plague.  On the plus side, we do have a female lamb in case our adult female dies.

Last part of Jugust is proving difficult.  A raid by the piggies that put 3 people in the hospital and now a black hive attack.
