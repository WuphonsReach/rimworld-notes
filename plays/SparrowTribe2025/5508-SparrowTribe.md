- Empath: Good for leaders.  Casts "Word of X" (joy, etc.), "Psychic soothe (gender)", "Bliss", "Mind wipe".

- Wildspeaker: Summon or affect animals.

# 5508

## Apr-5508

Five refugees from the mechanoid swarm.

Time to make Red the Elder this time around.  He has a burning passion for it.

## Jul-5508

So far we've saved one crash lander from Pact of Wasgaler, and two from Voidborn Federation.

## Sep-5508

Fair of Age -- It was boring.

Purple and Orange are working on the Protector psycast tree for healing, immunity and limb regrowth.

## Dec-5508

Green starts in on the Empath psycast tree.

Our first addition will be Wanysh, a lovesick yttakin.  She is age 29 with strong melee and animal skills.  But she's incapable of caring, shooting and social.  Her base melee is 12 from the torturer background plus agile fighter.  Her downside is the aggressive gene, which makes her prone to social fights.  If she had the ability to worship at the anima tree she would be great.

# 5509

## Apr-5509

Elder Remembrance held at dawn.  Unforgettable and everyone gets a major mood boost for 6 days.

Buch joins, he is a 9 year old child.  Red converts him to Ilwaban Creed (our ideology) after a few days.

Green will mind wipe Wanysh to convert her to our ideology.  She had a permament -12 due to the differences between our ideologies.

Buch gets his growth moment.  He's become zealous about our ideology.  

Entasga starts in on the technomancer psycast tree.  He's able to immediately boost the quality level of our two bolt-action rifles from poor/normal to good.

## Jul-5509

Entasga creates his first rock construct for hauling and cleaning around the colony.

I caught Purple and Wanysh getting some lovin' in the double bed roll.

## Sep-5509

Randy loves throwing raids at us.  I keep hoping for another bolt-action rifle or something better, but no luck so far.  At least one of the raids spawned in with manhunters (Raids go Wrong mod).

Entasga should be able to create his third rock construct soon.

With the last gasps of summer, we're setting up (3) growing zones for trees.  A 787 size one for popular, and a 628 and 539 sized pair for pine trees.  Both types trees produce 27 wood.  Pine trees grow in 20 days.  Poplar trees grow in 15.05 days.  I'm guessing that they'll take about 2 years to grow for the pine trees and 1.5 years for the poplar trees.  That should be about 4000 wood per year from the pine groves and 3500 wood per year from the poplar grove.

## Dec-5509

First blizzard of the season on the 2nd.

Entsaga reaches 168 heat capacity by the end of the season.  That's enough to run (8) rock constructs for hauling and cleaning.
